    About ARCHAEO-SCAN Atlas

    ARCHAEO-SCAN is an archaeological site discovery toolkit that combines remote sensing, historical documents, terrain analysis, and citizen science into a convergence-scoring system for identifying high-potential survey areas across the United States.

    Data Sources

    • SSURGO Soils (USDA)
    • NHD Hydrology (USGS)
    • NRHP Archaeological Sites
