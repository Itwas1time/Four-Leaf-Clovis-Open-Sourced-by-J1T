# Object material guide sources

Reviewed 2026-10-07. The native guide corpus has 62 authored entries and 186
fact bullets. Every fact carries its own citation IDs; the reader expands those
IDs to a source title and direct URL. No location scoring or site probability
is derived from these guides.

## Use and rights notes

The guide prose is original, compact paraphrase. It does not reproduce source
paragraphs, tables, images, object photographs, coordinates, or private names.
Factual statements are short and individually cited; SHA's historic bottle
education pages are copyrighted, so their manufacturing facts are summarized
in new wording and linked to the original pages.

Dates are presented with their source's scope. Yosemite's teaching ranges are
identified as broad park-guide ranges; ceramic mean dates remain tied to the
assemblage report; coin manufacture dates are not stated as dates of loss; and
isolated stone forms are not assigned a site, people, or probability.
