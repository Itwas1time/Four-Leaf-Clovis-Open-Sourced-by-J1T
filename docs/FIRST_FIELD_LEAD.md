# Montana field partnership lead (5 October 2026)

**Montana Big Belt Mountains, 2027 field season** is a concrete lead for
someone prioritizing fieldwork at documented sites with scope for additional
evidence. The [Archaeological Institute of America's project listing](https://www.archaeological.org/fieldwork/2027-us-az-big-belt-mt/)
says the team has 15 years of prior research, has identified multiple sites,
and plans to excavate two known multicomponent campsites while surveying the
surrounding landscape. Occupation spans from a few hundred to about 9,000 years
ago. The project names an archaeologist and the Helena–Lewis and Clark National
Forest as partners.

This is a **partnership lead**, not a Clovis model prediction or a claim that it
is the best site worldwide. It has a stronger immediate evidence basis than a
new location selected from the current Clovis heatmap, which lacks usable
survey and environmental layers. Specific finds, future research results, and
any investment or media rights remain unknown.

Before treating the project as an investment prospect, ask the directors and
land manager about the 2027 research design, permits, descendant-community
consultation, prior positive and negative survey results, curation, publication,
budget, and any lawful sponsorship or interpretation rights. Record the
resulting sourced observations in a candidate JSON file; do not assign a
priority score from the listing alone.
