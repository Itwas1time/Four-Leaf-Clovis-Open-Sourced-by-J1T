## Worked funding path: Ancient Argilos

Argilos has the clearest published invitation for a discovery-minded supporter
in this small lead set. The [mission's financing page](https://argilos.net/financing-argilos/)
invites contributions and names co-director Jacques Perreault as the first
contact. Its [2027 program](https://argilos.net/inscription-au-stage/) describes
continued excavation and museum work. This establishes an inquiry route, not
an available investment, defined funding package, or expected financial return.

The [mission homepage](https://argilos.net/) names the Serres Ephorate and
Université de Montréal as collaborators, with the Canadian Institute in
Greece providing auspices. Their own pages provide separate paths to check
those roles: the [Greek Ministry's Serres Ephorate page](https://www.culture.gov.gr/en/ministry/SitePages/viewyphresia.aspx?iid=1695),
[Perreault's university profile](https://recherche.umontreal.ca/chercheur/is/in13581/),
and the [Canadian Institute's Argilos page](https://www.cig-icg.gr/argilos/).
These pages do not certify a 2027 excavation permit or promise that any of the
institutions will accept a proposed grant.

A concrete first proposal would ask the directors for one research question,
the approved 2027 work area and authorization, an itemized need for recording,
analysis, conservation, or publication, a reporting schedule, and the custody
and public-access terms for resulting records. The directors and competent
Greek authority must confirm which permissions and agreements apply before
funding fieldwork. Clovis has not checked those project-specific documents.

Blue Creek is a different kind of published route: Maya Research Program has
a [general donation page](https://www.mayaresearchprogram.org/styled-10/) and
separately advertises [2027 field-school fees](https://www.mayaresearchprogram.org/styled-4/Excavate%20a%20Maya%20city.html).
Neither page defines a restricted research grant or financial return. Confirm
the exact site, purpose, budget, and restrictions with the project before
earmarking support.

Before committing funds, obtain the team's current research design, the
authorization relevant to the proposed work, land access, consultation and
community arrangements, custody and conservation plan, publication duties,
and a restricted budget with reporting milestones. These requirements differ
by jurisdiction and sometimes by the site's protection status. The linked
authorities and project directors must confirm how they apply to a particular
proposal.
